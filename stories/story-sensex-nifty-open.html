Sensex, Nifty open modestly higher on Tuesday as early gains stay thin

TOI 09:21 open prints and Moneycontrol mid-morning readings show small advances; Titan, Eternal, Adani Ports and HDFC Bank among early Sensex gainers

First filed: 22 Sep 2026, 10:14 AM IST · Last updated: 22 Sep 2026, 10:25 AM IST
CurrentWire · paraphrased from packet sources

TOI at 09:21 IST put Sensex at 74,945.01, up 86.02 points or 0.11%, and Nifty at 23,458.00, up 42.25 points or 0.18%, against Monday closes of 74,858.99 and 23,414.30. Indian cash equities opened modestly higher on Tuesday 22 September, with early gains remaining thin across the first hour. The Times of India liveblog at 09:21 IST put the Sensex at 74,945.01, up 86.02 points or 0.11%, and the Nifty at 23,458.00, up 42.25 points or 0.18%, against Monday closes of 74,858.99 and 23,414.30. Moneycontrol around 09:46 IST showed the Sensex near 74,967.70, up about 0.15%, and the Nifty near 23,457.45, up about 0.18%, with market breadth positive even as some of the opening advance was pared and IT names featured among early laggards. Those readings are sequential early-session snapshots, not a finished day.

The source backdrop was split. TOI noted crude still trading above $100 a barrel, while Moneycontrol pointed to cooler US yields and Asian technology stocks as supportive into the Indian open. Later TOI colour around 10:10 IST named Titan, Eternal, Adani Ports and HDFC Bank among early Sensex gainers. Levels here are point-in-time open and mid-morning prints that can move after the cutoff.
