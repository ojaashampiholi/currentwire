JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon said at the J.P. Morgan India Investor Conference that spending across the hyperscaler AI ecosystem had risen from around $300 billion last year to about $700 billion this year and could reach $1 trillion next year. In remarks published by CNBC-TV18 at 07:10 IST on Tuesday 22 September, Dimon said companies cannot judge AI only through conventional return-on-investment maths because adoption may become "table stakes" for customer experience. The figures are his stated estimates, not audited hyperscaler capital-expenditure totals.

He said the investment wave adds to capital demand alongside infrastructure, remilitarisation and government deficits, and could lift near-term inflation through hiring, factories, equipment and power buildout, which might keep interest rates higher for longer even if AI later proves deflationary via productivity. He also said new model providers and chip technologies could emerge over five to ten years, and that a market correction is possible without being certain AI spending would cause it. The comments are a CEO assessment delivered in Mumbai, separate from market-open stories that only use overnight Nasdaq strength as backdrop.
