India take on Sri Lanka for Asian Games women's cricket gold at Korogi Sports Park from about 10:30 IST on Tuesday 22 September, with live coverage on Sony Sports / LIV, according to the TOI cricket packet. Shafali Verma's century earlier in the competition has already given the Indian camp a headline moment to lean on heading into the decider, the kind of knock that travels with a side into a final even when the next match is a fresh toss.

As of 02:22 IST the final had not yet been played, so the medal colour remains unwritten. For a side that has treated this tournament as unfinished business until the last match, Tuesday morning is simply the appointment that matters: one game at Korogi under the Sony Sports / LIV cameras, one colour of medal, and a Sri Lanka outfit that will not gift it away on the biggest stage of the week.
