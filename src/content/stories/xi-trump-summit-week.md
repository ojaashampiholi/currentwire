Chinese President Xi is due in Washington for a summit with President Trump around the week of 24 September, according to China MFA and Reuters material in the overnight packet. Officials have given careful answers when pressed on rare-earth related questions ahead of the visit.

The meeting would be a high-visibility test of U.S.-China diplomacy at a moment when strategic minerals sit near the centre of trade and security talks. Watch the final travel confirmation, summit agenda signals, and any change in tone on rare-earth export policy.
