The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation approved an 8-km Pise to Panjrapur water tunnel, The Times of India reported. The project is framed as a municipal water-infrastructure upgrade for the Mumbai supply network.

Watch tender and construction timelines once BMC publishes next steps, and whether the tunnel is presented as part of a wider monsoon-resilience or supply-security package for the metropolitan region.
