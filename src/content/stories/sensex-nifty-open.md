Indian cash equities opened modestly higher on Tuesday 22 September, with early gains remaining thin across the first hour. The Times of India liveblog at 09:21 IST put the Sensex at 74,945.01, up 86.02 points or 0.11%, and the Nifty at 23,458.00, up 42.25 points or 0.18%, against Monday closes of 74,858.99 and 23,414.30. Moneycontrol around 09:46 IST showed the Sensex near 74,967.70 (+0.15%) and the Nifty near 23,457.45 (+0.18%), with market breadth positive even as some of the opening advance was pared and IT names featured among early laggards. Those readings are sequential early-session snapshots, not a finished day.

Background colour split by source. TOI noted crude still trading above $100 a barrel, while Moneycontrol pointed to cooler US yields and Asian technology stocks as supportive into the Indian open. Later TOI colour around 10:10 IST named Titan, Eternal, Adani Ports and HDFC Bank among early Sensex gainers. Levels here are point-in-time open and mid-morning prints that can move after the cutoff.
