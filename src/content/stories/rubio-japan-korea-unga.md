On the sidelines of the UN General Assembly on 21 September, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio met Japanese Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi and South Korean Foreign Minister Cho Hyun, according to Straits Times and Korea Times coverage of the APAC packet. The three issued a joint statement affirming US extended deterrence commitments to Japan and the Republic of Korea, opposing economic coercion and arbitrary export restrictions, and welcoming trilateral economic-security consultation among Washington, Tokyo, and Seoul.

The statement also stressed peace and stability in the Taiwan Strait and Korea denuclearisation, the same reports said, placing those themes alongside the deterrence and economic-security language rather than as afterthoughts. The consultations sit immediately ahead of the Trump-Xi state visit week in Washington, giving the trilateral message a clear timing context without disclosing any negotiating outcomes from those later bilateral meetings. No verbatim quotes beyond the joint-statement themes were available in the packet, so this brief sticks to the themes listed above.
