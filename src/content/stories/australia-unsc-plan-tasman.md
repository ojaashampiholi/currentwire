Australia is pursuing a United Nations Security Council seat for 2029 to 2030 and advancing the Plan Tasman navy arrangement with New Zealand, according to pm.gov.au and Reuters material in the overnight packet. The diplomatic bid and the bilateral naval plan are moving on parallel tracks.

A successful UNSC campaign would raise Canberra's multilateral profile, while Plan Tasman deepens Anzac defence cooperation at sea. Watch campaign endorsements from other UN members and any concrete shipbuilding or deployment milestones under Plan Tasman.
