Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index opened about 0.51% higher on Tuesday 22 September at about 25,170, according to Sina Finance and Sing Tao Headline market opens, while the Hang Seng Tech Index was up about 1.01% at the open. Technology names including Tencent, Alibaba and Xiaomi were reported higher in early trade. The move followed Monday's Wall Street session, in which the Nasdaq Composite rose about 2.3% and the S&P 500 about 1.5%, lifting risk appetite into the Asian cash day.

ScanX and CNBC-TV18 morning notes separately described wider Asia gains, including South Korea's Kospi up more than 2% and Japan's Nikkei 225 up about 1.4% in available session readings. Those regional figures are supporting colour, not a claim that Asia has finished the day. The Hang Seng levels here are point-in-time opening or early-session prints, distinct from the ASX open already covered in the prior packet, and live values can change after the cutoff.
