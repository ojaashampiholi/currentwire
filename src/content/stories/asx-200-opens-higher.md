Australia's S&P/ASX 200 opened higher on Tuesday 22 September and was up about 0.5% near 8,771 at roughly 5:40 am IST, according to ABC and the Australian Financial Review. Technology shares gained about 1.1% shortly after the open, while industrials added 0.8% and healthcare rose 0.7%, ABC reported. Technology was the strongest of the three cited sector moves at that stage. The early advance followed a strong US session in which the Nasdaq closed at a record, up about 2.3%, and the S&P 500 gained roughly 1.5% amid a semiconductor rally.

Oil supplied a mixed signal for the Australian market. Brent crude briefly slipped below US$100 before moving back near that level. The decline helped the broader mood but weighed on Australian energy names, tempering gains within the local benchmark. The index level and sector moves are a point-in-time snapshot from early Tuesday trading, not closing figures, and do not establish a rally across Asian markets.
