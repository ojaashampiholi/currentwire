Houthi forces are pushing to expand control across Yemen's highlands, Reuters reported. The advance adds pressure in a conflict theatre that already shapes Red Sea shipping risk and wider regional security calculations.

A New York Times account said President Trump had called off airstrikes connected to the fighting. Reuters has not verified that claim and did not independently confirm it. Packet material does not settle whether U.S. strike plans were altered, paused, or misreported. The call-off should be treated as attributed and Reuters-unverified.

On the ground, highland gains can change supply routes, local alliances, and the bargaining power of parties to Yemen's war. In Washington and allied capitals, any suggestion of a strike stand-down, verified or not, feeds debate over deterrence and escalation management. The territorial push itself remains the firmer operational fact.

Treat the Houthi highland advance as the confirmed thread. Treat the Trump airstrike call-off report as Reuters-unverified until U.S. officials or additional outlets corroborate it. Watch highland front lines and any formal White House or Pentagon comment.
