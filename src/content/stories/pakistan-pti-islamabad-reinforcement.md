As of Tuesday 22 September 2026, authorities are reinforcing Islamabad ahead of a Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf march planned for 27 September demanding the release of imprisoned former prime minister Imran Khan. Geo, citing police sources, reported that Sindh is sending about 1,950 personnel expected to reach the capital Tuesday night and that Punjab will provide about 19,000 personnel over the next two days after a federal request that, according to the same sources, sought 23,000 from Punjab. Those troop and police figures are sourced claims, not independently verified totals. Overnight, long lines of containers were being welded together at locations across Islamabad to restrict movement during the protest. Islamabad Police have detained several PTI parliamentarians under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance; Geo listed six, including MNA Adil Bazai, with reports saying detainees were shifted to Jhelum Jail.

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi have said the march will proceed and have described protest as a democratic and constitutional right. Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has warned that Islamabad will not be allowed to remain shut for prolonged periods because of demonstrations and said the government would implement Islamabad High Court orders if the PTI proceeds. The IHC has directed that public roads and other facilities should not be obstructed. The government frames its measures as public-order protection; the party criticises arrests and container barriers ahead of the planned mobilisation.
