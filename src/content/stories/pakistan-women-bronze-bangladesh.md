Pakistan Women sealed Asian Games 2026 cricket bronze on Tuesday 22 September, beating Bangladesh Women by 31 runs at Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan. LiveMint around 10:05 IST reported that Pakistan won the toss, chose to bat and posted 145 for 8 in the allotted overs. Bangladesh's chase closed at 114 for 7 in 20 overs. Sharmin Akter finished unbeaten on 46, a solid hand that still left the required rate out of reach. ABC and CricTracker published the same scoreline, so the third-place playoff result is triple-sourced rather than a single-outlet flash.

The bronze contest finished ahead of the day's headline fixture, the India versus Sri Lanka gold medal final scheduled around 10:30 IST at the same Asian Games women's cricket programme. India won that toss and elected to bat first. For this packet the gold match is upcoming context only: start time and toss are known, and no gold medal result is available by the 10:14 IST cutoff.
