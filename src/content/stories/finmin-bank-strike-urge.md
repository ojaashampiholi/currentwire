The Finance Ministry has urged employees of public sector banks and regional rural banks to refrain from a strike planned for 28 to 30 September, warning that industrial action during half-yearly closing on 30 September could disrupt services. Coverage from CNBC-TV18 at 08:27 IST and LiveMint noted that a midweek strike followed by the weekend could amount to an effective five-day interruption. The ministry asked unions to resolve outstanding issues through dialogue and to keep banking services uninterrupted. The call is an appeal to hold back, not a statement that the strike has been called off.

The United Forum of Bank Unions is pressing for a five-day banking week and for withdrawal of performance-linked incentive arrangements. The government side says PLI is already in abeyance, treating that as one demand met, while the five-day week remains under conciliation. Unions staged a one-day strike on 11 September and have proposed an indefinite strike from 26 October. The live dispute for this slice is therefore whether staff heed the ministry's refrain request ahead of the 28-30 September dates, not any claim that industrial action has ended.
