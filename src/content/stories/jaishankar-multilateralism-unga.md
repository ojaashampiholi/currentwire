External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar told the inaugural Partners for Multilateralism Summit on the sidelines of the 81st United Nations General Assembly that the global order is under stress from extreme competition, geopolitical tensions and actual conflicts, and that multilateralism must assert itself strongly. Challenges spanning peace and security, climate action, emerging technologies, development finance, counter-terrorism, supply chains, disaster resilience and pandemic preparedness all depend on a will to cooperate, he said, according to Asianet Newsable (ANI), Zee News and Times Now wires stamped about 06:00 to 07:00 IST on Tuesday. He warned that the "four F crisis" of fuel, food, fertiliser and finance, his phrasing, cannot be left to itself, and urged frameworks and guardrails for new capabilities alongside stronger advocacy and practice of reformed, more participative multilateralism.

During the same UN high-level week he also met Swiss Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis and Nauru President David Adeang, after attending the first Visegrad Group (V4) plus India foreign ministers meeting. Those bilaterals and the V4 session are secondary context to the summit remarks, which form the news of this slice. The stress-on-the-order framing and the attributed 4F warning are the points to carry forward in continuing UNGA coverage.
