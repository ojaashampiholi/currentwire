GIFT Nifty traded with a positive bias in Tuesday morning dealing on 22 September as Indian traders awaited the 09:15 IST cash open. CNBC-TV18's live markets blog, updated at about 08:05 IST, said GIFT Nifty was trading roughly 50 points higher and framed global cues as supportive after Wall Street's technology rally and cooler crude. The Nifty 50 had closed Monday at 23,414.30 and the Sensex at 74,858.99. Separate earlier DSIJ pre-market commentary had described GIFT Nifty near the mid-23,500s as context only.

Tuesday is also NSE IPO allotment day after the roughly Rs 22,568-crore offer was subscribed about 5.7 times, according to earlier exchange-linked reporting cited via The Times of India, with listing still scheduled for 24 September. Allotment outcomes are calendar fact for this slice unless final results are published. Cash Sensex and Nifty opening prints remain unverified at this cutoff and are not invented here. This item is pre-open risk colour, not a cash-market open story.
